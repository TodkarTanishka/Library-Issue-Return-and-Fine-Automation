MMCOE Library Rules & Regulations

01

Keep personal belongings at the Property Counter before entering.

02

Institute ID Card is mandatory for all library transactions and entry.

03

Maintain strict decorum, silence, and peace within reading areas.

04

Reference materials (dictionaries, handbooks) are for reading room use only.

05

All overdue books incur a automated fine of Rs. 6.00 per book per day.

06

In case of lost or damaged books, replacement cost must be paid by the user.
